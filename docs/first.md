# Manifesto

I want to build a website for my new alpine club in cooperation with my school "HTL-Leonding", I want to explore
possible benefits from having a website like better and easier organization or easier sing up and information for
members or people who want to sing-up. If this project flourishes and becomes real i want the website to serve the
purpose of informing active members about events (what equipment is needed, what experience is needed, where the location is, ..)
and also inform about more general topics like alpine safety or proper handling of trash or the laws around wild camping.
But I also want the website to be a place where interested students can learn about the alpine club and what we do.
There also needs to be a clear way to get in contact with me or other members.

Possible future projects could include a partnership with "bergwerk mountain store" or other mountaineering merchants
to buy equipment at a reduced rate for the school. This would have a positive effect for the store because introducing
young people to the alps in safe and fun way might bring new future customers.

The organization of trips should be managed by a group of people experienced enough to make proper estimates of time and
experience needed. Those will have special access to the website to post new events which will be visible to anyone regardless
if that person already is a member or not. Then after the trip happened it will be archived and saved for future reference
or as a kind of trophy.

The social potential of such a community should not be ignored, there is enormous potential for clans or groups which 
could compete in a number of categories such as accumulative elevation gained or highest peak claimed. This website 
could serve as a leaderboard or as a display board of a group's or individual's achievements. 
This could spark a real passion in people and really get students to pursuit a healthy lifestyle of adventure next
to the stress of daily school life.
