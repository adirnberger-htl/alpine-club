# User Stories

---

**alpine club - member**

As a member of the alpine club I want to be able to see when events such as hiking or climbing / bouldering trips happen and what equipment and or experience I need.

As a member I want to be able to get in contact with the trip organizers to ask potential questions or sing up for the trip.

As an active member I want to be able to educate myself on topics like alpine safety, tips and tricks as well as guidelines and laws surrounding the alpine area.

As a member I want to take part in adventures to the mountains without having to worry about safety.

As a member I want to be able to join a team of my choosing to go on trips with and count towards my team's total elevation.

---

**alpine club - team leader**

As a team leader I want to be able to post new trips in the form of events.

As a team leader I want to be able to have an easy way to add the elevation of a trip to the teams total. [specs](../tech-specs/system-reqs.md)

---

**new viewer** (potential member)

As a newcomer I want to easily see what the alpine club has done in the past (in form of an event archive).

As a potential member I want to have an easy way to sing up for the alpine club.
