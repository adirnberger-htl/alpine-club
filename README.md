# HTL Leonding Alpine Club Website
A web platform built in cooperation with HTL Leonding to streamline club organization, promote alpine safety, and inspire students to explore the outdoors.
Core Features

    Event & Trip Management: Experienced leaders publish upcoming trips with clear details on required equipment, experience levels, and locations. Events are visible to all students.

    Trip Archives: Completed expeditions are saved for future reference and to showcase club achievements.

    Easy Sign-Ups & Contact: Streamlined registration for new members and clear communication channels to reach club organizers.

    Alpine Education: Centralized guides on topics such as mountaineering safety, proper trash handling, and wild camping laws.

    Leaderboards & Competition: Track individual and group achievements—such as cumulative elevation gain and highest peaks claimed—to encourage healthy, friendly competition.
